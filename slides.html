The Problem

~45%of couples argue about money at least occasionally
(Fidelity, 2024)
22%of small nonprofits can even produce a 990-category spending breakdown
(Urban Institute)
~20 hrsa month small-business owners spend on compliance
(LegalZoom, 2026)

Three different people, one shared problem: tracking shared/regulated money is tedious and error-prone, so it gets done badly or not at all.

What's New

  • One unified rules/permissions engine across groups, nonprofits, and businesses — not three separate products
  • A Rules Checker that catches a violation proactively (over budget, wrong grant category, goal behind pace) — not just records a transaction
  • A Tax Prep Pack that organizes real tax-time paperwork (990 filing tier, donor letters, sales-tax worksheet) — not something Splitwise, Mint, or YNAB do at all
  • Its own cost (Costs page) and security posture (Security Log) visible to the user, not just the developer

Agent Architecture

Python computes every number and enforces every rule; AI only reads, sorts, and explains.

Safety

12/12 real attacks blocked: prompt injection, impersonation, PII leakage, tool-call loops, lockout, oversized/wrong-type uploads. New this round: 2-factor login + encrypted receipts at rest.
